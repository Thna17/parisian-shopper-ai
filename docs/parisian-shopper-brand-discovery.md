# Parisian Shopper brand discovery — preliminary review

Reviewed 4 November 2026. Source: the user-provided [Parisian Shopper Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/ParisianShopper), including its profile, featured items, visible photo grid and a small sample of recent posts. This is an initial, qualitative review—not a complete historical scan, a performance analysis, or an approved brand guide.

## Observed

- The profile mark is red and white and includes a Paris-inspired monogram and the Parisian Shopper name. Exact colors and original logo assets have not been verified.
- The visible product image grid uses predominantly white backgrounds, a clear product and box, compact branding and conspicuous prices. Featured collection posts can include more product details.
- The page uses conversational Khmer, familiar forms of address, emojis, pre-order details and invitations to contact the page. A formal-only tone would not capture the full observed voice.
- Content includes product collections, pre-order information, live selling and customer-service information. These are distinct formats, not interchangeable templates.
- A live session was visible, but this review did not establish a representative video style, pacing, soundtrack or performance.

Examples reviewed: [featured pre-order collection](https://www.facebook.com/ParisianShopper/posts/pfbid02aJhVbroJXVeU8Z15i9dwC8p218PZNKV8udpu17Xwk7rrjwvwGa7cDRX1X3aKDsGzl) and [featured customer-service post](https://www.facebook.com/ParisianShopper/posts/pfbid0PhJSQHQYSEQGuEDPUUJ61YL7XfDpWmS7DYjT8q9oujC942ZPRqUexYjc8gWPgvFwl). Links and content may change or require Facebook access.

## Proposed direction for human review

Use warm, helpful, conversational Khmer; clear factual product information; real approved product imagery; restrained white product-card layouts with red brand accents; and content-type-specific variations. The observation of past prices, pre-order times or return terms is not authorization to reuse them: use current verified source data.

Do not infer exact colors, font licenses, logo variants, usage rights, conversion impact or a comprehensive video style from this sample. Business owners should review a wider, representative set, confirm which work best reflects the desired brand, provide approved logo/fonts/media and approve any policy before use. Record the evidence and ownership for each example.

## Current application limitation

The app has both `BrandProfileVersion` and `BrandRuleVersion`. Brand Profile management and its brief are separate from the `BrandRuleVersion` pinned by content jobs and creative scenes. Changing or activating a Brand Profile alone does not update generation or enforce poster styling. Bridging them requires a versioned, auditable mapping and explicit policy for historical and in-flight work; do not silently replace pinned rules. Video has provider contracts but lacks a complete end-to-end creation and review workflow.
